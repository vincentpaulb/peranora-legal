PeraNora Support

Help for PeraNora, the local-first personal finance tracker.

Need help?

Open a support request and include your device model, iOS version, PeraNora app version, and a short description of what happened.

Open a support request

Do not include your transactions, receipt images, PIN, backups, or other financial data in a support request.

Common questions

  • Your finance data is stored on your device.
  • Use the export features before changing devices or resetting local data.
  • You can manage camera, photo library, notification, and Face ID permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Receipt scanning suggestions should always be reviewed before saving a transaction.
