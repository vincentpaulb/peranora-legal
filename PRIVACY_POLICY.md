# PeraNora Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

PeraNora is a local-first personal finance tracker. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

## Information stored on your device

PeraNora stores the information you enter in the app on your device. This can include your profile name, transactions, budgets, goals, wallets, bills, debts, categories, finance spaces, preferences, optional PIN lock setting, and receipt images. PeraNora does not upload this information to a PeraNora server.

Receipt scanning uses on-device text recognition to suggest transaction details. You should review every suggested value before saving it.

## Network services

PeraNora may connect to the following services:

- An exchange-rate service when you choose to refresh currency rates. The app sends no finance records to this service.
- A PeraNora update configuration file to check whether an Android app update is available. This check is not used on iOS.
- Apple or Google services when their platform APIs are used, such as local notification permissions, app distribution, or Android purchases.

As with any internet connection, these services may receive standard technical information such as your IP address and request metadata. PeraNora does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or cross-app tracking.

## Permissions

PeraNora requests access only when you use a related feature:

- Camera access to photograph and scan receipts.
- Photo library access to attach receipt images.
- Face ID or device biometric access to unlock the optional privacy lock.
- Notification permission to show bill, debt, budget, and update reminders.

You can deny or revoke permissions in your device settings. The related feature may be unavailable until permission is granted, while the rest of the app remains usable.

## Exports and sharing

You control exports, imports, and sharing. Backup files, CSV files, reports, and shared files can contain the data you selected for export. Protect exported files and share them only with people or services you trust.

## Retention and deletion

Your PeraNora records remain on your device until you edit, delete, export, or reset them. You can use Reset Local Data in Settings to remove local app records. You can also remove receipt images that are no longer attached to transactions.

## Children

PeraNora is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when PeraNora changes. The latest version will be published at the privacy policy link shown in the app and in its App Store listing.
